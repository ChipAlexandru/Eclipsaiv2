# Honold shopping preview

The demo at `/honold-demo` uses a static snapshot of the public [Honold online shop](https://shop.honold.ch/shop/). On 19 September 2026, the seven listing pages contained 134 simple products with one displayed CHF price each. The extractor captured all 134 product names, shop URLs, category labels, displayed prices, and main Honold photos. The [official brand site](https://www.honold.ch/) supplies the logo; the [locations page](https://www.honold.ch/standorte/) confirms Honold Erlenbach at Seestrasse 69, 8703 Erlenbach.

Regenerate the snapshot from the repository root using `node scripts/extract-honold-catalog.mjs`. The script rejects variable products and incomplete listing data, and fails if any photo cannot be downloaded. `catalog-report.json` records counts and limitations.

Coverage is the seven public `/shop/` listing pages. The shop promotes **Gutscheine** separately; they are not in this snapshot. Product detail ingredients, allergens, custom options and checkout prices were not collected. A listing does not establish store stock or pickup availability. The deterministic branch quantities in `stockAdapter.mjs` support this demo scenario only. The basket and pickup preview never call Honold, reserve products, take payment, or create an order. Honold must confirm prices, ingredients, allergens, and availability before any real purchase.

The compact presenter control offers three fictional demo scenarios: First visit, Regular and Gift. It sits outside the Honold shopping experience, stays collapsed during shopping and is the only place where a presenter can advance order status. The storefront then shows one available matching featured Honold product and offer, a compact category strip, available recommendations first and a two-column mobile catalogue. Product cards use uncropped photos with names, effective prices, original prices for offers and add controls. The bottom dock keeps voice and basket actions prominent; catalogue discovery stays with categories and the voice assistant.

The interactive review supports illustrative pickup and local delivery flows. Pickup offers two example branches and 15-minute slots in Europe/Zurich; delivery uses a fictional address and deterministic two-hour windows. After the one-step order confirmation, pickup shows Confirmed → Preparing → Ready with an order-specific QR identity and pickup number. Delivery shows Confirmed → Packing → On the way without a collection QR. Branch hours (daily 08:00–19:00), 30-minute pickup lead time, delivery address and windows, stock quantities, slot capacity, offer rules, QR identity and progress events are **synthetic**, not Honold operational data. Public list prices are never changed; the customer offer appears as a separate saving. Confirmation revalidates time, stock, capacity and fulfilment details. Basket, customer, branch, slot, delivery address or delivery-window changes clear any prepared review. No customer data, notification, payment, reservation, delivery or order is created.

Product details expose the sourced public name, photo and list price. Clearly labelled demonstration gift-message and wrapping choices appear only for an explicit stable set of gift products. The fictional profiles include sample history, usual orders and recommendations. Single-product offer prices are indicative; the exact basket review applies eligibility, rounding and caps. Approval is a separate explicit step bound to that review, and produces only a simulated payment and sample order. Sample orders are versioned in browser local storage and isolated by fictional profile; reorder rebuilds the basket against current sample stock and public snapshot prices and requires a new review and approval.

Voice requires a configured server-only `HONOLD_OPENAI_API_KEY`, browser microphone access, and a secure context. The browser receives only a short-lived Realtime client secret from the Honold token route. Touch browsing and basket preview work without voice.

## Public deployment

Available without a password at `https://www.eclipsai.com/honold-demo`. Voice is enabled only when that deployment has a dedicated server-side `HONOLD_OPENAI_API_KEY`; the shared `OPENAI_API_KEY` is never used as a fallback. Touch shopping and category browsing remain available without voice. All checkout/order state stays in the browser; no real payment or fulfilment integration is connected.
