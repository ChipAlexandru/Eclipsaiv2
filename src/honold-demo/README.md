# Honold shopping preview

The demo at `/honold-demo` uses a static snapshot of the public [Honold online shop](https://shop.honold.ch/shop/). On 19 September 2026, the seven listing pages contained 134 simple products with one displayed CHF price each. The extractor captured all 134 product names, shop URLs, category labels, displayed prices, and main Honold photos. The [official brand site](https://www.honold.ch/) supplies the logo; the [locations page](https://www.honold.ch/standorte/) confirms Honold Erlenbach at Seestrasse 69, 8703 Erlenbach.

Regenerate the snapshot from the repository root using `node scripts/extract-honold-catalog.mjs`. The script rejects variable products and incomplete listing data, and fails if any photo cannot be downloaded. `catalog-report.json` records counts and limitations.

Coverage is the seven public `/shop/` listing pages. The shop promotes **Gutscheine** separately; they are not in this snapshot. Product detail ingredients, allergens, custom options and checkout prices were not collected. A listing does not establish store stock or pickup availability. The deterministic branch quantities in `stockAdapter.mjs` support this demo scenario only. The basket and pickup preview never call Honold, reserve products, take payment, or create an order. Honold must confirm prices, ingredients, allergens, and availability before any real purchase.

The opening storefront shows a selected demo customer, a matching featured Honold product and category offer, a category strip, and an entry to pickup planning. Product cards use uncropped photos with separate names, category labels, prices and add controls. The interactive checkout scenario offers two illustrative branches, transparent category-specific offers, 15-minute pickup slots in Europe/Zurich, and a manual Received → Preparing → Ready tracker with a demo code. Branch hours (daily 08:00–19:00), 30-minute lead time, stock quantities, slot capacity, offer rules, and progress events are **synthetic**, not Honold operational data. Public list prices are never changed; the customer offer appears as a separate saving and the result is labelled an example total. Slot selection and confirmation revalidate future time, stock and capacity. Basket, customer, branch or slot changes clear any confirmed preview. No customer data, notification, payment, reservation, or order is created.

Product details expose the sourced public name, photo and list price plus clearly labelled demonstration gift-message and wrapping choices. The fictional profiles include sample history, usual orders and recommendations. Single-product offer prices are indicative; the exact basket review applies eligibility, rounding and caps. Approval is a separate explicit step bound to that review, and produces only a simulated payment and sample order. Sample orders are versioned in browser local storage and isolated by fictional profile; reorder rebuilds the basket against current sample stock and public snapshot prices and requires a new review and approval.

Voice requires a configured `OPENAI_API_KEY` on the server, browser microphone access, and a secure context. Touch browsing and basket preview work without voice.

## Public deployment

Available without a password at `https://www.eclipsai.com/honold-demo`. Vercel deployments disable voice in both the page and token route, regardless of any shared `OPENAI_API_KEY`. Local development can still use the existing voice setup. Touch shopping and catalogue search remain available. All checkout/order state stays in the browser; no real payment or fulfilment integration is connected.
